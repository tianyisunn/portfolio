Almond Blossom | Bachelor Graduate Exhibition

Interior & Furniture Design

A furniture collection inspired by Vincent van Gogh’s Almond Blossom, exploring the intersection of classical post-impressionist art and contemporary functional living.

The Challenge: Bridging the gap between high-art symbolism and everyday functional furniture. While modern trends favor utility, there is a lack of systematic approaches to translating artistic heritage into ergonomic spatial elements.
The Solution: A five-piece signature collection (Coffee Table, TV Console, Lounge Chair, Sofa, and Cafe Table). Through meticulous element extraction, material contrast, and linear abstraction, the project transforms Van Gogh’s visual rhythm into a cohesive, production-ready interior system.
Methodology & Research: The process integrated user psychological surveys, visual semiotics (deconstructing "Almond Blossom"), iterative sketching, and high-fidelity 3D prototyping to ensure aesthetic warmth without compromising structural integrity.

Timeline: March – July 2025

Tools: Procreate, AutoCAD, 3ds Max, Blender, Figma

Up | Community Activity Center Design

Spatial & Narrative Design

A transformative child-centric community space inspired by the Pixar film Up, translating themes of adventure, friendship, and discovery into a physical landscape that stimulates imaginative play and social connection.

The Narrative Drive: Rather than a literal recreation, the design deconstructs "Paradise Falls" into abstract visual cues—clouds, balloons, and woodland motifs. These elements act as a psychological catalyst, encouraging children to embark on their own "lifelong promises" through exploration.
Functional Programming: The space is strategically zoned by activity flow: transitioning from quiet reading nooks and craft zones to active play spaces. High-fidelity spatial layouts prioritize safety and child-scale accessibility without sacrificing the whimsical atmospheric narrative.
Technical Execution: Utilizing exploded isometric diagrams and detailed functional area mapping, the project ensures a seamless transition from a "fairytale atmosphere" to a viable, high-performance community facility with optimized circulation for both children and parents.

Key Concept: Adventure as a Learning Tool

Tools: AutoCAD, 3ds Max, Photoshop, V-Ray

MODUCATE | Modular Play-as-Learning System

Social Innovation & Product Design

A multi-sensory modular system designed to empower underprivileged children through tactile safety education, developed through field research and rigorous market benchmarking.

Evidence-Based Design (User Research): The project was driven by primary field research and interviews with "left-behind children." Findings revealed a critical gap in traditional safety education, where abstract warnings fail to resonate. MODUCATE fills this void by translating life-saving knowledge into a tangible, "toy-like" medium that encourages self-led discovery.
Competitive Benchmarking: Extensive analysis of existing educational toys showed a dominance of purely digital or non-interactive static blocks. MODUCATE outperforms these by integrating sound, light, and magnetic feedback, offering a superior "Phygital" (Physical + Digital) experience that prioritizes both engagement and child safety standards.
Human-Centric Interaction: Utilizing magnetic connections and real-time sensory responses (visual and auditory), the system lowers the barrier to entry for diverse age groups, ensuring that complex concepts like energy conversion and fire safety are intuitive and accessible.

Research Method: Field Interviews, User Personas, Competitor Analysis

Impact: Socially-conscious Design for Safety Literacy

Modular Furniture | Children's Seating Solution

Product Design & Fabrication

An ergonomic, tool-free assembly seating system designed for outdoor recreational spaces, bridging the gap between digital parametric design and physical timber fabrication.

The Concept: Inspired by the turning pages of a book, the design features ergonomic curves that provide essential spinal support for children. The modular interlocking system allows for seamless adaptation across diverse environments, from public playgrounds to indoor learning spaces.
Prototyping & Fabrication: Demonstrating strong "Design-to-Make" capabilities, the project moved beyond 3D modeling into physical production. The final prototype was realized through CNC machining and manual wood finishing, ensuring structural stability and safe, splinter-free surfaces for young users.
Sustainable Logic: Constructed from eco-friendly, weather-resistant timber, the seat utilizes a "slot-in" mechanical joint system. This reduces the need for metal fasteners, simplifies maintenance, and emphasizes a sustainable lifecycle for modern urban furniture.

Fabrication: CNC Machining, Manual Wood Finishing

Tools: Figma, AutoCAD, 3ds Max

Lacquerware Museum | Cultural Narrative Design

Visual Communication & Layout

A multi-dimensional editorial system designed for the Chengdu Museum lacquerware collection, deconstructing 2,000-year-old archaeological motifs into a modern interactive brochure.

The Narrative Drive: The design challenges the static nature of traditional museum catalogs. By creating a tiered layout system (A3 spread, circular inserts, and 16mo booklets), the project invites users to "unfold" the history of the boat-shaped coffins and rare lacquer artifacts through a progressive visual journey.
Visual Logic: Utilizing a high-contrast palette of "Lacquer Red" and "Obsidian Black," the design extracts and simplifies complex historical patterns into scalable vector motifs. This bridge between ancient semiotics and contemporary minimalism ensures the cultural content is accessible to a younger, design-conscious audience.
Production Feasibility: The project meticulously considers print finishing and tactile engagement. From the choice of paper texture to the mechanical interaction of the "circle-in-square" page folds, every element is designed for high-fidelity physical production, ensuring a premium "souvenir" experience for museum visitors.

Focus: Grid Systems, Typographic Hierarchy, Print Production

Tools: InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop

Morning C | Boutique Cafe Interior

Commercial Space & Engineering

A concept-to-detail commercial interior project blending American industrial aesthetics with highly optimized operational workflows.

Spatial Optimization: The design focuses on maximizing the utility of a boutique floor plan. Through detailed "Exploded Axonometric" analysis, the project partitions the space into distinct zones—open-bar service, high-turnover seating, and relaxed social corners—balancing customer experience with business efficiency.
Technical Feasibility: Moving beyond aesthetics, the project includes comprehensive construction drawings (floor plans, lighting schedules, and elevation details). The material logic—combining raw industrial metals with warm timber—is validated through high-fidelity rendering to ensure lighting and texture accuracy for real-world implementation.
Human-Centric Detail: Every custom element, from the "Morning C" wall typography to the integrated modular furniture, was designed with ergonomics and maintenance in mind, proving the design's readiness for high-traffic commercial environments.

Fabrication Logic: Construction Documentation, Lighting Design

Tools: AutoCAD, 3ds Max, V-Ray, Photoshop
