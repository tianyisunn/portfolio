01
Overview

A platform built for Dublin's living art scene

Artsy Dublin is a live web platform for discovering local performance and arts events in Dublin — music, theatre, film, ballet and beyond — with recommendations shaped around personal interests.

The project combines event discovery, personalised recommendation, and lightweight social connection. Instead of presenting culture as another long list of listings, the platform helps users decide what is worth seeing and who they might want to go with.

02
The Problem

Existing platforms aren't built for art people

Platforms like Eventbrite have reasonable event categorisation — but they're general-purpose. Anyone can create an event and tag it however they like, leading to unreliable, polluted category data.

①
Inaccurate tagging — user-generated events frequently carry wrong or misleading category labels.
②
Weak recommendation algorithms — existing platforms don't build a nuanced interest profile; suggestions feel random.
③
No spontaneous social layer — social features focus on long-term groups, not lightweight, event-specific buddy-finding.

"I want to find someone to go to a jazz night with — not join a Facebook group about jazz."

03
The Solution

Curated sources + social layer + personal diary

Rather than relying on user-generated tags, Artsy Dublin sources event data directly from reputable venues and institutions — concert halls, cinemas, galleries — keeping quality high and noise low.

The final product turns that data into a calmer browsing experience: users can explore cultural events, keep track of what they have attended, and use social features only when they are useful.

A

Event Buddy Matching

See who else is going to an event and send a lightweight "join me" request. No long-term commitment required until you want one.

B

Art Event Diary

Log events you've attended. Add a short reflection, a rating, a photo. Your diary becomes both a keepsake and the basis for your recommendation profile.

C

Tailored "For You" Feed

Recommendations built from your interest profile, diary history, and what your connections are attending — not generic "popular in Dublin" filler.

D

Community-Led Events

Informal cultural gatherings such as art-topic presentation nights, debate clubs, and collaborative viewing parties.

Find Browse Dublin arts events through clearer categories and interest-led discovery.
Remember Turn attended events into a personal cultural record through the art diary.
Connect Meet around specific events instead of joining broad, long-term social groups.

05
Design

Warm, tactile, cultural — not another cold tech app

The visual direction was deliberate: Dublin's art scene feels warm, textured, and human. We used cream, deep brown, and forest green to echo printed programmes, gallery labels, and the atmosphere of small cultural venues.

#5A3713 Primary / Deep Brown
#10755A Accent / Forest Green
Readable first Event details are arranged for quick scanning across mobile and desktop.
Soft social cues Buddy-finding supports the event journey without overpowering it.
Consistent tone The interface feels like a cultural guide rather than a generic ticketing product.
06
Technical Stack

A data-driven concept with a responsive React front-end

The project required the team to think beyond static screens: event sourcing, recommendation logic, profile state, and responsive interaction all had to work together as a coherent product.

Event Sourcing

Structured around verified Dublin venues and cultural institutions, reducing the noise of open user-generated listings.

Recommendation Engine

Designed around user interests, diary history, and social connections so personalisation can improve over time.

Front-end

A component-driven React interface with responsive flows for discovery, profiles, diaries, and social features.

Content Management

User profiles, diary entries, event logs, social connections — all managed through a structured backend data layer.
