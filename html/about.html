About me

I explore both physical design and digital design

I’m Emma, a designer working across both spatial and digital experiences, with a particular interest in UX design and frontend development. My background began in product and interior design, where I developed an understanding of how people move through, interact with, and emotionally respond to physical spaces. Over time, that curiosity naturally expanded into digital experiences, leading me toward interaction design, user experience, and creative frontend development.

They call me Emma the Explorer

I enjoy translating ideas into interfaces that feel intuitive, engaging, and human-centered, combining research, storytelling, and visual thinking with implementation. Originally from Chengdu, I’m interested in cultural exchange and the way different perspectives shape how people communicate, behave, and experience design. Outside of work, I’m often exploring galleries and parks, observing everyday interactions, or collecting small details that later find their way into my creative process. Now based in Dublin, a place where is famous for Guinness, but just between us, I prefer Irish coffee.
